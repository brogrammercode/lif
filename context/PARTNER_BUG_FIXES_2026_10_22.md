## Postal directory

- Source: [GeoNames India postal export](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/IN.zip), downloaded 22 October 2026; [format and licensing](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/readme.txt).
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit is included in the app and generated source.
- The checked-in subset contains 20,609 distinct state/district/PIN combinations across 36 states/territories. It is served by API V2 without a paid service, key or third-party runtime lookup.
- Names are title-cased; Orissa/Pondicherry aliases are normalized and Leh/Kargil are assigned to Ladakh. Postal district names are used as city choices, not as a claim about statutory municipal boundaries. Source data may retain historical district names.

## Follow-up consistency audit

- Separate state, city and PIN fields now share selections by category and address key prefix. Changing an editable parent clears editable dependent answers; approved answers remain intact. Saved city/PIN selections restore when their geography is unambiguous. Ambiguous values require an explicit selection.
- The API checks the complete address combination, including separate fields, and prevents incompatible new field types for semantic selectors and formatted identifiers.
- Optional dropdowns can be cleared. Invalid legacy selections need correction before submission. UPI handles cannot end in punctuation; semantic values are trimmed before storage.
- Activation email retries and SMTP workers use conditional delivery claims. A retry cannot reset an active send; abandoned claims, including legacy claims without timestamps, become recoverable after five minutes. SMTP acceptance still cannot guarantee inbox delivery or exactly-once delivery after a provider/network failure.
- Failed admin refreshes block mutations until refreshed successfully. Application decoding also validates required flags and normalizes optional category, user and confirmation metadata.
- Address selection changes always rebuild dependent controls. Discarded upload previews are evicted when workflow data reloads, as well as after upload, replacement and disposal.
